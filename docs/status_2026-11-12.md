# Status 2026-11-12: latency matching, a second policy family, and collection without markers

Follows [status_2026-11-07.md](status_2026-11-07.md). No robot result here is counted: no policy has a success rate.
No robot runs were made after 7 November.

## 1. Execution on the robot (6–7 November)

- **Latency matching.** Following UMI, the delay from command to motion was measured with small chirps sent through
  the robot bridge and cross-correlated with the measured joints: arm L 58 / R 64 ms, jaw L 173 / R 171 ms (the jaw about
  110 ms later than the arm, which matched the observation that the robot closed its jaws late). Camera delays, measured
  by filming the host clock: fixed camera 1.4 ms, left wrist 38 ms, right wrist 61 ms. With matching on, arm and jaw
  commands are sent ahead by their delays and the policy state is taken at the capture time of the fixed-camera image.
- **p_grasp on the robot.** The grasp classifier trained on the human jaw gives P(held) 0.97 / 0.94 on the robot's
  closed, empty jaws (the robot jaw looks larger and glossier), so on the robot p_grasp comes from the jaw state until a
  classifier for the robot jaw exists.
- **Joint-limit hold.** When inverse kinematics is pinned at a joint limit and far from the target, that arm holds its
  last joints instead of moving to a twisted closest pose.
- **Contact floor (not used).** A torque-change detector near the table was built and switched off: near the table the
  arm sags 2–6 mm below its command and the torque change is not yet separable from gravity; joint torques are now
  logged to fit a gravity model first.

## 3. A second policy family: X-VLA on the flattening data (10–12 November)

- Init: our SoftFold X-VLA checkpoint (650k steps), new domain id 22, task "Flatten and align the T-shirt.". Data: the
  0.3× set converted to relative 32-step chunks in the X-VLA convention (same rows and videos, per-sample loss masks).
  Continuous jaw target, jaw state masked, no gradient clipping, FP32. One epoch := 178,219 samples (a third of the
  534,657 frames).
- 5 epochs on 2× RTX 4090, global batch 16: training loss 0.6 → 0.07.
- 12 November (running): warmup-stable-decay continuation to epoch 100 on 4× H100, global batch 64, learning rate 1e-4,
  on 330 demonstrations (the 301 training demonstrations + 29 tag-free ones of 11 November; 586,436 frames; val 53,
  test 49). The plan is to add each day's data with recent days over-sampled and the normalisation frozen.
- No offline or robot result yet.

## 4. Collection without markers (11–12 November)

- **Why it should work.** The policy acts in relative motion with a task-anchor state; absolute marker poses are not
  needed for its labels, only a start pose and a heading.
- **What changed.** All AprilTags were removed (both hand units and the table start tags). Each demonstration starts with
  both units resting on black L tapes. Start pose from the tapes and the resting units in depth; heading from the wrist
  IMU (Mahony filter) anchored at rest; the rest of the chain is unchanged.
- **Check.** A tagged take (20261105_164113) processed as if it had no tags, against its own tag measurements: jaw-tip
  error left 40 → 17 mm and right 80 → 9–14 mm after two seeding errors were fixed (the seed rotation was undefined before
  the trimmed demo start; the rigid fit started 4–8 cm outward of the true body origin), flat over demo time.
- **Data.** 11 November: train 29, val 7, test 4, silver 16, reject 4 (the pilot take is test only). 12 November (four takes
  processed so far): train 16, val 5, test 2, silver 12, reject 4.
- **Camera.** On 12 November the fixed camera was re-aimed by hand after re-cabling; the residual was measured by
  registering the static depth scene against takes of the previous day (control pair: 0.02° / 1.5 mm) and folded into the
  calibration (`robot_cam_v007`). Uncorrected, the error would have been 6–27 mm in the workspace.
