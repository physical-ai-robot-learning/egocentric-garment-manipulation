No robot result in this document is counted: no flattening policy has a success rate.

## 1. FLAT149: the UMI recipe (2–3 November)

- Data: 149 flattening demonstrations, 48 of 30 October and 101 of 2 November (a demo was kept only if the shirt ended
  flat in the fixed-camera frame); Cg trajectories, 0.5× time-stretch, continuous jaw target (servo).
- Recipe: the cloth-folding recipe of the UMI appendix, item by item: 100 epochs, learning rate 3e-4 (vision encoder
  3e-5), DDIM with 50 training and 16 inference steps, image augmentation matching UMI's random crop (zoom 0.05, shift
  0.025), rotation ±5° and colour jitter (brightness 0.3, contrast 0.4, saturation 0.5, hue 0.08). The jaw is learned as
  a continuous value (no BCE head) and binarised at deployment. Initialised from the H100 checkpoint 663012 of the
  GOLD-281 flattening fine-tune; 4 H100 GPUs.
- Hue: implemented as a rotation of the chroma plane in YIQ space (one 3×3 matrix). The library hue adjustment cost
  about 62 ms per camera sample on the CPU data workers and made the run data-bound at about 3 s per update.
- Offline (`val_score_mm`, defined in [status_2026-11-02.md](status_2026-11-02.md)): best validation checkpoint epoch 80
  (step 012160, 60.89 mm), best test checkpoint epoch 90 (step 013680, 64.36 mm). In the deploy checkpoint list; no counted
  robot result.

## 2. Deploy changes (3 November)

- `amp`: a motion-amplitude scale that shrinks each committed step's translation and rotation about the current pose
  (the user found the motion too large).
- `start_root`: the start move can use the medoid start of any dataset; the FLAT149 starts lie 19 mm (left) and 31 mm
  (right) from those of GOLD-281.
- `cam_align`: compares the live fixed-camera view with a reference day.

## 3. Camera re-aimed, new collection, split by tracking gaps (3–4 November)

- The fixed camera was re-aimed during a take on 3 November. That take and later ones use the re-solved calibration
  `robot_cam_v006` (anchor tags 400 / 401 + table plane, `scripts/pose/robot_cam_from_anchors.py`; the two coplanar
  anchor tags give a planar PnP ambiguity, so every IPPE solution is tried and the one with the camera highest above
  the table is kept). Data before and after the move are kept apart.
- 3 November: 7 takes, 100 demonstrations. 4 November: collection continued; one take with a disconnected right jaw
  motor lost 3 demos; the left jaw was re-assembled between takes and its servo reading now falls when the jaw opens
  (`scripts/pose/jaw_from_motor.py` detects the flipped direction per take).
- Trim: both ends of every demo while both arms are slower than 3 cm/s for 0.5 s, margin 0.3 s.
- Split (user decisions of 4 November, latest wins): the longest point-tracking gap is measured inside the trimmed
  window. **train** = GOLD with a longest gap below 0.25 s (first 0.2 s, raised to 0.25 s the same evening); GOLD with a
  longer gap alternates between **val** and **test**; SILVER whose trimmed window has no tracking gap at all → train,
  other SILVER → **silver** set (auxiliary robustness evaluation); REJECT → **reject** set (analysis only). One gapped
  GOLD demo was moved to train after the user reviewed its video. Timelines and images are never cut.
- Dataset build: 0.3× time-stretch, label F, continuous jaw; positions inside a tracking or marker gap of at least 0.3 s
  are masked in the loss (`FILL_MASK_S=0.3`, `--mask-mode gated`); training sets `MASK_CROSS_GAP=1`: once one future
  step of a predicted chunk is masked, every later step of that chunk is masked too.
- Code: `scripts/flatten_builds/` (trim audit, gap audit, split, per-take build, QA renders),
  `src/garment_pipeline/labels_dataset/tag_rig_unsplit.py`, `src/garment_pipeline/training/tagrig_common.py`.

## 5. Grasp held / empty labels and p_grasp (5 November)

- Why: the jaw servo is read with torque off, position only: no load or current, and the closed position does not
  separate cloth from nothing. A closed jaw does not say whether the cloth is held; the cue has to come from the wrist
  camera.
- Events: jaw close events of 343 flattening demos, segmented with a tighter hysteresis for this pipeline only (open
  above 0.30, closed below 0.15 of the jaw range, dwell 0.1 s); the earlier segmentation hid 148 partial re-opens.
- Gold: the user labeled close events blind to any prediction (held / empty / uncertain) in 4 rounds: 585 events, of
  which 543 map onto the new events (held 372, empty 171).
- Classifier: ResNet-18 on a crop around the jaw tips of the wrist image, take-grouped 5-fold cross-validation: held
  precision / recall / F1 0.90 / 0.90 / 0.90, empty 0.79 / 0.77 / 0.78 (per event).
- Causal p_grasp(t) at 15 Hz: mean per-frame probability over the last 0.4 s, restarted at each jaw state change. In the
  same cross-validation: held events median 0.99 (12 % of held events dip below 0.2 at least once), empty events
  median 0.03 (17 % touch 0.9 at least once).
- Frozen generator `grasp-v2-W0.4` (trained on all gold, so p on gold events is in-sample). Per-frame side file for a
  LeRobot set without rebuilding it.
- FLAT-PGRASP (started 5 November, 17:50): the FLAT250 recipe, fresh from 663012, but the masked jaw slots of the state
  carry p_grasp of the left and right hand instead of 0 (`PGRASP=grasp-v2-W0.4`), on the data of 3, 4 **and** 5 November:
  **train 301** demonstrations (534657 frames), **val 46**, **test 45**. Offline after epoch 1: val 79.45 / test 78.86;
  after epoch 2: val 68.79 / test 68.43 (not comparable with FLAT250: different held-out sets). The deploy does not
  compute p_grasp yet.
- In the evening of 5 November a comparison of the time-stretch was being set up: the same p_grasp recipe at 0.3× on
  rented GPUs and at 1.0× on the cluster. No result yet.
- Code: `scripts/grasp/`.
