# Status 2026-11-16: X-VLA catches up with the DiT offline; more data without markers

Follows [status_2026-11-12.md](status_2026-11-12.md). No robot result here is counted: no policy has a success rate.
No robot runs were made after 7 November.

## 1. Question

On 12 November a second policy family, X-VLA initialised from our SoftFold checkpoint, was being trained on the
flattening data. Does a large pretrained vision-language-action model reach the error of the DiT trained for this task,
when both are measured on the same held-out demonstrations?

## 2. Data (to 13 November)

- Demonstrations recorded without markers are now part of training. A demonstration goes to training when its longest
  tracking gap is under 0.6 s; other good ones alternate between validation and test.
- 11 November, regraded: train 43. 12 November (10 takes): train 52, val 9, test 4, silver 8. 13 November (5 takes): train 18, val 6, test 5, silver 10,
  reject 1.
- Set used for the first run (3 to 12 November): 398 training demonstrations (729,215 frames), validation 61, test 53.
  With 13 November added: 416 training demonstrations (763,360 frames), validation 67, test 58. Normalisation statistics
  were frozen at the first set so that training could continue across sets.
- While building these sets, an older per-take conversion was found to have put training demonstrations into the
  12 November validation set; the sets were rebuilt and the run of 12 November was replaced.

## 3. Method

- State: both jaw-tip poses, both jaw openings and the grasp probability of each jaw (p_grasp), now in slots of its
  own instead of replacing the jaw openings. Actions: relative jaw-tip motion, 32 rows ahead, continuous jaws.
- First run: from the SoftFold checkpoint, full fine-tuning, bf16, global batch 256, cosine schedule to 100k steps.
- Continuation: from the 92k weights of the first run, a fresh optimiser, the learning rate warmed up again to 5e-5 and
  decayed over 108k steps, on the set that includes 13 November.

## 4. Check: offline error on the same anchors as the DiT

Position error of the predicted jaw tip 16 and 32 rows ahead, mean of both arms, on the validation / test sets of the
first run (30 demonstrations each; 4,189 / 3,811 anchors). "Hold" predicts no motion.

| | val, 16 rows | test, 16 rows | val, 32 rows | test, 32 rows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hold | 54.6 mm | 54.9 mm | | |
| X-VLA, first run, best (90k) | 49.0 | 48.8 | | |
| **X-VLA, continuation, best (84k)** | **36.7** | **36.3** | **64.9** | **65.6** |
| DiT FLAT-PGRASP 0.3× (same anchors) | 38.5 | 36.8 | 72.6 | 68.3 |

- The first run stopped improving at 49–50 mm once its learning rate was near zero; restarting the schedule on more
  data brought it to the DiT level at 16 rows and below it at 32 rows.
- The two models are scored by two evaluation programs; their hold baselines agree within 1–2 % (DiT program: 55.2 /
  56.1 mm), so the comparison is close but not exact. The 84k checkpoint was chosen on validation.
